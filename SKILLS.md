# JEFF Skills

JEFF can:

- Review structured Agent NFT proposals against identity, state, evidence, memory signals, and owner policy.
- Produce typed shadow decisions with calibrated confidence and reasons.
- Detect missing evidence, stale data, policy conflicts, transfer privacy risks, and unsafe authority assumptions.
- Create privacy-preserving review receipts that bind inputs and outputs by hash.
- Support proposal linting, transfer checks, benchmark evaluation, and human approval workflows.

These are decision and review capabilities. They do not include signing, transaction submission, publication, spending, credential custody, or autonomous execution.
